**Last updated: November 23, 2026**

This is the full Privacy Notice for the melvtech.com website and MelvTech's contact channels (form, WhatsApp, phone and email). It explains what data we collect, what we use it for, who we share it with, and how you can exercise your rights. This notice is governed by Mexican law, and the Spanish version is the reference text.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

**ESTRATEGIAS COMERCIALES Y SERVICIOS INTEGRALES-THE ROOTS** ("Roots"), a legal entity organized under the laws of the United Mexican States, with its address at Calle Segunda 213, Residencial Anáhuac, San Nicolás de los Garza, Nuevo León, C.P. 67140, Mexico, is the controller of your personal data under the Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (the "Law").

**MelvTech** is the trade name under which Roots handles requests for home automation, networks, alarms, video surveillance and monitoring for homes and businesses.

For any matter about your personal data, write to our Personal Data Desk: [atencion@melvtech.com](mailto:atencion@melvtech.com).

## 2. What personal data we collect

**When you write to us through the contact form:**

- Your name and email address.
- The service you are interested in, and whether it is a new project or an upgrade of an existing system.
- Your estimated budget and desired start date, if you choose to provide them.
- Your contact preference (email or phone) and the message you write.

**When you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email:** your phone number or email, your profile name, and the content of the messages you send us.

**When you browse the site:** technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you visit, and whether you click a WhatsApp link. This is collected through tracking technologies, as explained in section 6.

**Sensitive personal data.** We do not ask for it. Please do not include health data, ethnic origin, religious beliefs, sexual orientation or other sensitive data in your messages.

## 3. What we use your data for

**Necessary purposes** (without them we cannot assist you):

- Responding to your request or question.
- Preparing and sending you a quote, or proposing an assessment.
- Scheduling and following up on your request.
- Complying with applicable legal obligations.

**Additional purposes** (you can object without affecting the assistance you requested):

- Measuring how the site is used and how our ads perform.
- Showing MelvTech ads on Facebook and Instagram to people who visited the site.

If you do not want us to use your data for the additional purposes, write to [atencion@melvtech.com](mailto:atencion@melvtech.com) with the subject "Limitación de uso" (use limitation). You can also adjust your ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account, or block tracking technologies in your browser.

## 4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. To run the site and assist you, we use providers that process data on our behalf:

- **Meta Platforms** (Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp), through the Meta Pixel and for assistance via WhatsApp.
- **FormSubmit**, a service that receives what you write in the form and emails it to us.
- **Cloudflare** and our hosting provider, which deliver the site and process technical connection data.
- **Google (YouTube)**, only if you play a video embedded on the site.

Some of these providers are located outside Mexico, so your data may be transferred abroad. We may also hand over data to competent authorities when the law requires it.

## 5. Security measures

We apply reasonable administrative, technical and physical measures to protect your data against loss, unauthorized use or access. The site is delivered over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

## 6. Tracking technologies (Meta Pixel and videos)

This site uses the **Meta Pixel**, which collects technical information about your visit (pages viewed and clicks on WhatsApp links) and sends it to Meta. We use it to measure our ads and to show you MelvTech advertising.

Embedded YouTube videos do not load until you press play and use YouTube's enhanced privacy mode.

You can limit these technologies through your browser settings, with extensions that block trackers, or through your ad preferences on Facebook and Instagram.

## 7. Your ARCO rights and withdrawing consent

You have the right to **access** your data, to **rectify** it if it is inaccurate or incomplete, to **cancel** it, or to **object** to its processing (ARCO rights). You can also **withdraw** the consent you gave us.

To exercise them, send your request to [atencion@melvtech.com](mailto:atencion@melvtech.com) with the subject "Derechos ARCO" (ARCO rights), and include:

1. Your name and an address or means to receive notifications.
2. Documents proving your identity (or your legal representative's).
3. A clear and precise description of the data about which you want to exercise the right, unless it is a request for access.
4. The right you want to exercise, or what you are requesting.
5. Any other element that helps us locate your data.

If you request a rectification, also state what must be corrected and attach supporting documents.

We will respond within a maximum of **twenty business days** from the date we receive your request. If it is granted, we will carry it out within the **fifteen business days** following our response. These periods may be extended once for an equal period when circumstances justify it. Exercising ARCO rights is free of charge; only the costs of reproduction, copies or shipping may be charged.

## 8. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations. After that, we delete or anonymize it.

## 9. Minors

This site is aimed at adults. If we find that we collected data from a minor without the authorization of their parent or guardian, we will delete it.

## 10. Contracted services

This notice applies to data obtained through the site and the contact channels listed above. If you hire a service, this notice is complemented by the corresponding quote or contract.

## 11. Changes to this notice

When we change this notice, we will publish the updated version on this page with its update date. If the changes are significant, we will highlight them on the site.

## 12. Authority

If you believe your rights have been violated, you may go to the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (Ministry of Anti-Corruption and Good Governance), the authority for personal data protection held by private parties in Mexico.

## 13. Governing law

This notice is governed by the laws of the United Mexican States. For its interpretation and enforcement, the parties submit to the competent courts of Monterrey, Nuevo León, without prejudice to the rights the law grants to consumers.
