## How we started

MelvTech was born as a specialist in video surveillance: installing reliable security cameras so you can monitor your home or business in real-time, from wherever you are.

## Why we expanded our services

Over time, the same customers who trusted us for their cameras began asking us for more: to automate access and lighting, have a network that never fails, and alarm systems that connect with everything else. So we added home automation, networks, and mesh Wi-Fi, alarms, and active monitoring — not as a change of focus, but as a natural expansion of what we were already doing well.

## How we work

- We design each installation based on the actual size of your property, not with generic packages.
- We serve residential and commercial clients in Monterrey and its metropolitan area.
- We never set a price without first knowing your space, which is why we always invite you to request a quote.
- Our services work together: cameras, alarms, home automation and monitoring operate from the same place.
