At MelvTech, we expanded from being a specialist in video surveillance to offering complete solutions for businesses. With network infrastructure we design how your whole business connects: which switches to use, how to segment your network, and how to keep it organized as you grow.

We design and implement your office, business, or branch network—from managed switches to VLAN segmentation—for businesses in Monterrey and its metropolitan area.

## What this service includes

- Managed switch configuration
- VLAN segmentation
- Traffic separation between cameras, access control, administrative network, and guests
- Network topology documentation

The result is a network organized into segments, where a problem in one area does not affect the others.

## Benefits

- A safer network, with sensitive traffic separated from the rest
- Easier to scale without redoing the installation
- Better performance, because not everything competes for the same channel
- A single point of responsibility for making your whole network work together

## It integrates with the rest of your systems

Network infrastructure is the foundation of your other systems: cameras, access control and Wi-Fi connect to it so everything runs in an orderly way.
