At MelvTech, we expanded from being a specialist in video surveillance to offering comprehensive solutions for businesses. Structured cabling is the foundation of everything else: without a well-installed network, cameras, access control, and alarms don't work reliably.

We design and install your office, business, or branch's network cabling, organized and documented, for businesses in Monterrey and its metropolitan area.

## Installations for

- Offices and workspaces
- Businesses and commercial establishments
- Warehouses and industrial buildings
- Branches and sales points

## What this service includes

- Category 6 and 6A structured cabling
- Surface-mounted or conduit wiring, depending on the space
- Labeled patch panels and cable organizers
- Certification tests for each network point
