At MelvTech, we expanded from being a specialist in video surveillance to offering complete solutions for businesses. Access control is one of those areas: we help you decide who enters your office, warehouse, or branch, and at what time, without relying on physical keys.

We design each installation according to the size of your business and the number of access points you need to control, for businesses and offices in Monterrey and its metropolitan area.

## Types of controls we install

- Card or proximity reader
- Fingerprint reader
- Keypad with access code
- Combination of two methods for greater security

## Where we install

- Main entrance for offices and businesses
- Warehouses and loading areas
- Parking lots and vehicle barriers
- Restricted internal areas (servers, storage, cash register)

The result is a business where you know exactly who entered, when, and from where, without having to be present.

## Benefits

- You no longer depend on copying or recovering physical keys
- You can instantly deactivate access if someone stops working with you
- Entry and exit log by person and by door
- Restrict sensitive areas without constant supervision

## It integrates with the rest of your systems

Access control does not work alone: it connects with the cameras and alarms you already have or that we install with this service, so everything works from one place.
